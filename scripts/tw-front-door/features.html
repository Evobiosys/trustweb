What it does today

Features

Only what the app really does now. Try each one in the demo, with imaginary friends and nothing to set up.

Your web of connections

The people you have met, placed in three rings: close friend, friend, acquaintance. It lives on your phone. Connect in person with a QR code, which shows that you met, or with a link for people you are not meeting in person. Coming soon: a moving QR code. Coming later: NFC, Bluetooth and AirDrop.

Call into your web

Ask in plain words. The question goes to the people you are connected with, according to the ring each one sits in, and what comes back is only what they chose to share. Nothing is shared without a yes from the person who holds the note: given live, or given in advance for a line they marked that way. A decline looks exactly like no match, so a "no" gives nothing away.

Record a meeting

Record on your own, or in a meeting with someone: who, where, and a consent hint. "Listen for" prompts remind you what to note.

Transcribe and send a meeting

Notes become lines about a person. A transcript is made on your own device where it can be, otherwise later. Past meetings keep place, notes and transcript together. After a meeting, send its notes and transcript, encrypted, to someone you are connected with, or share them through the Meet code.

My Resources

What you could offer, lend or point to, kept as short lines. Requests that reach you wait in one place to review.

Encrypted Swiss backup

A backup is encrypted on your phone before it leaves. The app is hosted on Infomaniak in Switzerland, and the server keeps only ciphertext it cannot read.

Reach someone

Your address on TrustWeb

trustweb.one/<your-name> reaches you. Anyone who opens it lands in TrustWeb, creates their own web in one step, and appears on your "intending to connect" ring as "reached out" (green-blue). You decide what happens next.

You claim your name in the app (Settings, then username). If this website ever has a page of that name, the explicit form app.trustweb.one/<your-name> always works.

Nothing about the person reaches a server except the connect itself: the relay carries it, encrypted.
