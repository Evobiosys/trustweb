What it does today

Features

Only what the app really does now. Try each one in the demo.

Your web of people

The people you have met, placed in three rings: close friend, friend, acquaintance. It lives on your phone.

Record a meeting

Record on your own, or in a meeting with someone: who, where, and a consent hint. "Listen for" prompts remind you what to note.

Notes and transcript

Notes become lines about a person. A transcript is made on your own device where it can be, otherwise later. Past meetings keep place, notes and transcript together.

Inventory

What you could offer, lend or point to, kept as short lines. Requests that reach you wait in one place to review.

Ask your web

Ask in plain words. The question goes to the people you are connected with, according to the ring each one sits in, and what comes back is only what they chose to share.

Consent per ask

Nothing is shared without a yes from the person who holds the note: given live, or given in advance for a line they marked that way. A decline looks exactly like no match, so a "no" gives nothing away.

Demo accounts

Try it with imaginary friends, each labelled "dummy", and with up to four devices side by side. Demo accounts only see demo content by default.

Encrypted Swiss backup

A backup is encrypted on your phone before it leaves. The server in Switzerland keeps only ciphertext it cannot read.

Connecting in person or by link

Now: a QR code, to show that you met in person, and a link, for people you are not meeting in person. Coming soon: a moving QR code. Coming later: NFC, Bluetooth and AirDrop.

Send a meeting

After a meeting, send its notes and transcript, encrypted, to someone you are connected with, or share them through the Meet code.
