Where this is headed

Early, mid, and late game

The early game looks nothing like the late game, and the live demo you just tried is deliberately just the early game.

Early game · now

One connection at a time

Taking notes during high-trust encounters is how the web forms at all: one conversation, one note, one ring assignment. Nothing to query yet without this part first.

Mid game

Querying starts paying off

Once enough people are taking notes, asking the web starts finding things a single friend group never could: a question reaches further, still only through people who chose to be askable, still with a decline looking exactly like no match.

Late game

A vision many have had before us

A web of trust, information travelling only through relationships instead of a public platform, is not a new idea. PGP's own web of trust, and the ongoing "Rebooting the Web of Trust" workshops, worked on versions of this for decades. What's different now is the infrastructure to actually run it at the scale of a real social network, not just a keyring.
