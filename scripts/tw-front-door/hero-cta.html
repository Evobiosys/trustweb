What it can do today

  • Call into your web: ask your direct contacts a question in plain words; each of them decides whether to answer, and nothing is shared without a yes.
  • Keep the people you trust in three rings: close friend, friend, acquaintance.
  • Record and keep a meeting, with its notes and transcript.
  • Keep your resources: what you could offer, lend or point to.

Passing a question on further through friends is not built yet. It is the vision.
