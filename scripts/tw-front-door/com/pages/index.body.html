The hosted service

What we host for you

  • An encrypted copy of your recordings, kept on our server, so they are safe if your phone is lost and reachable from a new one.
  • Encrypted backups of your notes and settings.
  • The relay that lets your devices and your connections exchange what you chose to share.

Everything is encrypted on your phone before it leaves, so the server holds ciphertext it cannot read. The app is hosted on Infomaniak in Switzerland. Details are on the privacy page.

Smooth updates

We keep it current for you

The hosted version promises smooth updates and migrations: when TrustWeb changes, your notes and settings move along with it, and you do not have to do anything. An offline install is on its own: once you run it without us, we are no longer responsible for keeping it current.
