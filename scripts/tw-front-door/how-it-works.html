Two sides of one coin

How it works

Asking is what is new, so it comes first. It only finds something once notes exist.

I

Querying

Call into your web: ask a question in plain words. It travels through the rings of people who chose to make their notes askable, and the answer comes back showing how it was found.

A decline looks exactly like no match, so a "no" gives nothing away either.

+
II

Taking notes

Right after meeting someone, jot down what's worth remembering: what they're looking for, what they can offer, who they are. Voice or typed, on your own device, nothing sent anywhere until you choose to share it.

Place them in a ring, close friend, friend, or acquaintance, and choose who can even ask about each line.

Notes have to exist before a question has anything to find. Everyone starts on the notes side; the querying side only gets useful once enough of it is there.

Meeting in person and starting a connection on the spot is in the works.

III

Meet in person

When you meet someone new, they can share a QR code or a link on the spot. Scan it with your camera and the app opens with that person selected. Choose your ring (close friend, friend, acquaintance) and press "Add to my web" to connect. If they share a Signal or Molly link, one press opens your direct channel to them.

Or type their TrustWeb code, name, or address (a DID: a unique identifier that works without a server) instead of scanning.
