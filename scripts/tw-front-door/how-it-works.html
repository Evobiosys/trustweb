Two sides of one coin

How it works

Taking notes comes first: there's nothing to query until some notes exist.

I

Taking notes

Right after meeting someone, jot down what's worth remembering: what they're looking for, what they can offer, who they are. Voice or typed, on your own device, nothing sent anywhere until you choose to share it.

Place them in a ring, close friend, friend, or acquaintance, and choose who can even ask about each line.

+
II

Querying

Ask your web a question in plain words. It travels through the rings of people who chose to make their notes askable, and the answer comes back showing how it was found.

A decline looks exactly like no match, so a "no" gives nothing away either.

Notes have to exist before a question has anything to find. Everyone starts on the notes side; the querying side only gets useful once enough of it is there.
