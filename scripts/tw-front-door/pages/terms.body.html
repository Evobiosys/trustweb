1. The service

An experiment

TrustWeb is experimental infrastructure from EvoBioSys, in Vienna. It is offered as it is, without a promise that it is always available or free of errors.

2. Your content

It stays yours

Your notes, transcripts and recordings stay yours. If you use backup or hosting, we store them encrypted and only to give the feature back to you. Please record people only where you may.

3. The free allowance

Limits

Hosting is free up to the equivalent of 10 recordings of up to 1 hour each (10 hours in total) per person. A hosted recording that is neither written nor opened for 90 days may be removed. Beyond the allowance, hosting will be paid; the terms are still to be worked out.

4. Web results

Results from Brave

When you use "Show results here", you agree not to scrape, store, cache or redistribute the results. They come from Brave, and they are shown only to you, for the moment. Your search words go to Brave Search through our server, without your network address; see the privacy policy. Powered by Brave.

5. Fair use

What not to do

Do not use the service to harm or harass others, to store what you may not store, or to strain the server. We may limit or stop access that does.

6. Changes and contact

These terms will change

This is a draft. Applicable law and jurisdiction are still to be decided. Questions: connect@evobiosys.org.
