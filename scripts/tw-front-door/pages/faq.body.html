First questions

The questions a newcomer usually has, answered as TrustWeb works today. Where the honest answer is "not yet", it says so.

What is TrustWeb?

An app for your phone where you keep notes on the people you meet, place them in three rings (close friend, friend, acquaintance), and later ask your web a question in plain words. Your question goes to the people you are connected with, and each of them decides whether to answer.

Who sees what I write?

Your notes, transcripts and recordings stay on your phone. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to share it, for example by sending a meeting to a person you are connected with, or by making a backup. Both are encrypted on your phone first. Two optional services are starting soon: hosting your recordings (encrypted the same way) and transcription on the server, where the audio itself goes to the server to be turned into text. Hosting your recordings is encrypted the same way. Server transcription, if you choose it, is the exception: the audio itself goes to the server to be turned into text.

Who sees my ask, and can people say no?

Only the people you are connected with whose ring allows your ask. Each of them can share, or decline. A decline looks exactly like "no match", so a "no" gives nothing away.

Where does my data live?

On your phone first. If you make a backup, or send a meeting, or have your recordings hosted, an encrypted copy is kept on a server in Switzerland. The messages between phones pass through a relay on the same server, encrypted so the relay cannot read them.

What does the server know?

It stores ciphertext it cannot read, with sizes and dates. The relay can see routing details, such as which address a message is for and when it arrived, but not what it says. (The very first connection step between two phones, sent before they share a key, is the one message that is not encrypted.) Two exceptions read content: the demo inventory (see demo accounts) and, if you choose it, server transcription, which handles your audio. Details are on the privacy page.

What does it cost?

The app is free today. A server in Switzerland can host your recordings, free up to the equivalent of 10 recordings of up to 1 hour each per person. Beyond that it will cost something, and we are still working out what. See pricing.

How do I start?

Tap Demo to try it with imaginary friends, no setup. Tap Install to put the app on your home screen. On an iPhone, open the page in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

What is a demo account?

A pretend identity for trying TrustWeb: imaginary friends, each labelled "dummy", so you can ask, answer and see consent. By default, a demo account only ever sees demo content, never someone's real notes. One demo contact, "Demo inventory", is a small service on our server with made-up lines; an ask to it is also shown, with your demo name, to the person who runs it, who can answer it live. When you move to the live app, you can clear the demo contacts first.

Can I leave and take my data?

Your data lives on your phone, so it goes with you. Settings has a "Reset this phone" button that deletes everything TrustWeb stored on it, after a confirmation. An encrypted backup can be restored on another phone. A plain export of notes and transcripts as a text file is not built yet.

Is the code open?

The code will be published as part of EvoBioSys. The public copy is older than the live app. If you want to read the current code now, ask us.
