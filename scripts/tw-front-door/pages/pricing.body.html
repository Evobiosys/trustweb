Pricing

Free while we learn. This is experimental pricing, and we want to hear what would be fair.

Experimental, first version, 2026-10-06

Free

The app, and a server in Switzerland that holds your recordings

The app itself costs nothing today. On top of that, a server in Switzerland can host your recordings for you, so a copy is kept off your phone. The free allowance is the equivalent of 10 recordings of up to 1 hour each (10 hours in total) per person.

Hosting is starting soon (experimental). Until it is switched on, your recordings stay on your phone.

Hosting is switched on (experimental).

Hosted recordings are stored as ciphertext the server cannot read. A recording that is neither written nor opened for 90 days may be removed.

Beyond the allowance

It will cost something

Past the free allowance, hosting will be paid. We have not set any price yet, and we will not publish one until we have heard from people. Paid plans are being worked out.

Server transcription

Coming, terms to be worked out

Today a transcript is made on your own device where that works. Transcription on the server is coming, and what it will cost, and what is included for free, is still to be worked out.

Switched on (experimental), terms to be worked out

A transcript is made on your own device where that works. If you choose it, the server can transcribe a recording instead (the audio then goes to the server, see the privacy page). What it will cost, and what is included for free, is still to be worked out.

Marketplace fee

Planned: 1 % when a transaction runs through us

This is not built yet. The idea: when two people who trust each other do a transaction through TrustWeb, we take 1 %, and in return it is easier for both sides. See Marketplace.
