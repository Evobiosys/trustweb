Updates and migrations

Hosted or on your own

  • The hosted version promises smooth updates and migrations: when TrustWeb changes, your notes and settings move along with it.
  • An offline install is on its own: once you install it and run it without us, we are no longer responsible for keeping it current.

Server transcription

Coming, outside providers via OpenRouter, see privacy

Today a transcript is made on your own device where that works. Transcription on the server is coming: it will run through outside providers via OpenRouter, and it is free while we test: up to 10 hours of audio per person, within a daily budget shared by everyone. It is switched off today. How it works and what it means for your audio is on the privacy page.

Switched on (experimental), outside providers via OpenRouter, see privacy

A transcript is made on your own device where that works. If you choose it, the server can transcribe a recording instead, through outside providers via OpenRouter (the audio then leaves your phone, see the privacy page). It is free while we test: up to 10 hours of audio per person, within a daily budget shared by everyone.
