History

Versions so far, and what comes next

TrustWeb grows in small, proven steps. Each version below was shown working with real people before it got its number. The app shows its version under Settings > About.

v0.0.1 · summer 2026

The Alm

The first version, made and tried at the Alm: one phone asks, one phone answers, and nothing is shared without a yes. The question travelled, the data stayed. Everything since builds on that one rule.

v0.1 · September 2026

The first three dots that worked

Three people on the rings, connected for real through the relay, asking and answering across phones. Proven with Max: a question from one phone reached another person's phone and came back answered, with every answer sealed so that a no and a nothing look the same.

v0.2 · October 2026

The Web Curator and Tana sync

Your web of connections became something you curate: the Web Curator sorts the people you met into rings, from a meeting, a note or a scan of your own notes. Tana sync (and your Obsidian or Logseq vault) feeds the people you already wrote about into the app, and writes ring changes back.

v0.2.1 · October 2026

The Android app, with notifications

TrustWeb as an app on Android: the same app as on the web, and it can tell you when something arrived while it was closed. Someone wants to connect, someone is sending a query, a message: each has its own switch under Settings > Notifications. The relay only wakes your phone with an empty push; what arrived is read on your phone, under your keys. Download the Android app (APK).

v0.3.0 · planned

Your devices in sync

Continue on the phone what you started on the laptop, and the other way round. The Tana sync then runs on a machine of yours that is on anyway, a server or a desktop, so the phone receives what it needs without doing the work.
