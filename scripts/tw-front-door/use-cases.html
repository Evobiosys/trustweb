What could stand on it

Use cases

TrustWeb is an infrastructure: the layer under things people already want to do. Below is what it can enable. Each card says honestly what exists today and what is still planned. For when this kind of tool fits best, see where it is specifically useful.

Notes side works today, pilot planned

Events

At a gathering you meet many people and remember few. Note what someone looks for or offers, and ask your web afterwards. A first pilot with EA groups is planned (see For EA groups).

Works today, one hop

Introductions

Ask who knows someone. The person in between decides whether to pass your question on, so an introduction stays warm. Longer chains are in the works.

Coming soon

Marketplace

Where people already trust each other, a transaction can run through TrustWeb. When it does, we take 1 %, which makes the exchange easier for both sides. Nothing of this is built yet.

In the works

QuestHub

QuestHub organizes resources for community quests. TrustWeb can be the trust layer under it: who may ask, who may answer.

Works today in the app

Sharing resources

Tools, a cargo bike, a spare room, an hour of skill, lent between people who trust each other, below the value where a public listing pays off.

In the works

Info sharing as curations

People curate what they know, a pediatrician, a reliable mechanic, a good cleaner, and share it with the rings they choose instead of posting it publicly.
