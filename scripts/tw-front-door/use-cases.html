Use cases

TrustWeb is an infrastructure: the layer under things people already want to do. Below is what it can enable. Each card says honestly what exists today and what is still planned. For when this kind of tool fits best, see where it is specifically useful.

Introductions

Ask the people you are connected with who knows someone, and each decides whether to answer. Passing a question on through a friend, so an introduction stays warm, is in the works.Your question can travel on through a friend, so an introduction stays warm: you choose how far (one hop by default).

Works today, pilot planned

Events

At a gathering you meet many people and remember few. Afterwards, call into your web to find who was looking for something or offered it; a few notes written after each conversation are what it finds. A first pilot with EA groups is planned (see For EA groups).

Works today in the app

Sharing resources

Tools, a cargo bike, a spare room, an hour of skill, lent between people who trust each other, below the value where a public listing pays off.

Planned

Finding housing

A room, a flat or a sublet found through people who trust each other. Trust is what makes it possible: the person offering knows who is asking, and the asker knows who vouches. Not built as its own flow yet; today you can call into your web in plain words.

Coming soon

Marketplace

Where people already trust each other, a transaction can run through TrustWeb, which makes the exchange easier for both sides. Fee: 80 percent of what others charge. Nothing of this is built yet.

In the works

Coordinating Quests

Communities organize resources for shared quests: who needs what, who can help. TrustWeb can be the trust layer under it: who may ask, who may answer. See how it is done on the quest platform.

In the works

Info sharing as curations

People curate what they know, a pediatrician, a reliable mechanic, a good cleaner, and share it with the rings they choose instead of posting it publicly.
