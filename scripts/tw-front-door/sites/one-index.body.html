The app

Ask the people you trust.

TrustWeb keeps the people you have met in rings on your phone. Ask in plain words, and each person decides whether to answer. Nothing is shared without a yes.

Open the app Install Demo

On Android you can also take the Android app (APK). The demo uses imaginary friends and needs no set-up.

Your rings

Place the people you have met in three rings: close friend, friend, acquaintance. They live on your phone.

Call into your web

Ask a question. It goes to the people you are connected with, and a decline looks exactly like no match.

Your notes stay yours

Record a meeting, keep notes, share a line only with the rings you choose.

Your address is trustweb.one/<your-name>: anyone who opens it can reach out to you, and you decide what happens next.
