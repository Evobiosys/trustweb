The steward of TrustWeb

Looking after the code, so it stays in the open.

TrustInTheWeb.org exists to steward the TrustWeb code: to keep it a commons, to decide in the open how it grows, and to make it possible for others to build on it.

The app: trustweb.one

Our intention

Stewarding the code

Software that holds the names of the people you trust should not belong to a company that can change its mind. We hold the TrustWeb code on behalf of everyone who uses it or builds on it, and we commit to keeping it open under the licences below.

We do not run the app for you; that is what TrustInTheWeb.com does. Keeping the two apart is deliberate: the steward of the code is not the seller of the service.

Who decides

Governance

TrustInTheWeb.org sits under EvoBioSys, and we intend to turn it into a foundation. Until then, decisions about the code are taken by the people listed under About us, with their advisors; we intend to publish them where anyone can read them.
