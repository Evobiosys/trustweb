Hosting and services

Hosting for TrustWeb, so a group does not have to run a server.

TrustInTheWeb.com is a commercial public-benefit company. It hosts trustweb.one and the Web Curator for groups and organisations; both are stewarded by TrustInTheWeb.org. The app works on a phone on its own. If you would rather have us keep your recordings and look after the updates, this is the hosted service.

The hosted service

What we host for you

Encrypted recordings

A copy of your recordings on our server, so they are safe if a phone is lost and reachable from a new one.

Encrypted backups

Your notes and settings, encrypted on your phone before they leave.

The relay

The service that lets your devices and your connections exchange what you chose to share.

The server holds ciphertext it cannot read. The app is hosted on Infomaniak in Switzerland. Details are on the privacy page.

Free while we test

The allowance

Hosting is an experiment, and it is free while we test: up to the equivalent of 10 recordings of up to an hour each (10 hours in total) per person.

Hosting is starting soon (experimental). Until it is switched on, your recordings stay on your phone.

Hosting is switched on (experimental).

Smooth updates

We keep it current for you

When TrustWeb changes, your notes and settings move along with it, and you do nothing. An install you run without us is on its own: once it runs without us, we are no longer responsible for keeping it current.

Prefer to run it yourself?

Run it yourself for free

Hosting is optional. TrustWeb is open source under the AGPL, the app costs nothing and keeps everything on your phone, and you can run your own server from the source. Where to find the code, and what the project is, is on TrustInTheWeb.org.
