In short

Privacy

The server holds ciphertext it cannot read. For an AI summary, what you ask (and, for a summary, your question with the matches shown) goes through trustweb.one to a computer run by TrustInTheWeb, is answered there and is not stored. No analytics, no tracking cookies, no outside fonts. Full details on the privacy page.
