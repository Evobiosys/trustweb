A pilot for communities

For EA groups

TrustWeb is an open-source app that lets people ask their community's notes a question. After a conversation you save a few notes; someone in the group asks; the holder sees the question, not the asker, is alerted once a day only on a match, and approves or declines (a decline looks like “nothing found”). Notes stay on each phone, and you can import them from your own Swapcard export.

We start with EA groups, run a test event in April 2027, and plan an opt-in pilot at EA Global London (May 2027). Record only what you would happily pass on in a warm introduction; anyone can have items about them deleted.
