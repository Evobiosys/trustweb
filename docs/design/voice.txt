TrustWeb voice (on demand; loaded by a button in the styleguide, never with the page)

Tone
  One person speaking to another, in sentence case. Plain verbs. Nothing sells, nothing cheers.
  Warm by being specific, not by adjectives.

Person
  You and your people by name. The app never says "we", never "the system", never
  "automatically". When something happened without the person doing it, say who or what caused
  it: "Lena shared this with you."

Buttons
  Say what happens: "I can drive", "Ask Lena to introduce", "Share with first ring". The same
  words carry through: a button "Share" ends in a line "Shared."

Never
  Scores, streaks, counts about a person, "trust level 3", badges, "verified", exclamation
  marks, apologies in errors, "Oops". Private means invisible, so no "locked" or "restricted"
  wording either.

Empty and error states
  An empty screen says what can happen here and offers the first step. An error says what went
  wrong and the one thing to do next. Neither has a mood.

Source
  Design laws and ring vocabulary come from the UX decision log in the primary repo
  (docs/10-ux-decisions.md). This text extends it; where they differ, the log wins.
