1 WHAT THE PROGRAMME IS (facts, sources at the end)
- Legal basis: Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act) Art. 6(9): a gatekeeper provides end users, and third parties authorised
  by them, effective, continuous and real-time portability of data provided or generated by the user, free of charge.
- Meta's implementation: "Data Portability" through Export Your Information (EYI), built on the Data Transfer Project (DTP).
- PUSH, not pull: Meta does not hand a third party a read token for Facebook/Instagram. The third party is a DESTINATION: it runs an
  OAuth 2.0 authorization server and an import API; Meta's infrastructure calls that API on the user's behalf, once or on a schedule.
- Consequence for TrustWeb (local-first, no central data store): the importer must be a TrustWeb-hosted endpoint that receives the
  user's data and hands it to the user's own device only (end-to-end: encrypted to the device key, deleted from the server after
  pickup). It must never become a store of people's Facebook data. This is the design constraint for the build after approval.

4 AUTH FLOW (we are the authorization server)
 - OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow. Meta redirects the user to our /authorize; the user signs in to TrustWeb (device key /
   passkey) and consents per category; we issue a short-lived code -> access token (minutes) + refresh token (recurring transfers:
   daily or monthly, the user chooses). Scopes per DTP type, e.g. photos.write, posts.write (destination-side write scopes).
 - Revocation URL: offered; revoking stops recurring transfers at once.
 - Tokens are bound to one user and one device key; no token gives read access to anything in TrustWeb.

6 DRAFT ANSWERS (pre-approval form)
 Q: What does your app do?
 A: TrustWeb is a local-first contact and trust network: people keep their contacts, notes and resources on their own devices and
    share them only with people they trust. Users want to bring their own Facebook and Instagram posts, photos and events into it.
 Q: Why do you need these data types?
 A: To let a person carry their own posts, photos and events into their own TrustWeb, where they decide again, item by item, who may
    see them. Nothing is published automatically and nothing is used for advertising or profiling.
 Q: Where is the data stored?
 A: On the user's own device. Our EU server only relays the transfer, encrypted to the user's device key, and deletes it after the
    device picked it up (at most 7 days).
 Q: Security standards?
 A: TLS 1.3 only; at-rest encryption of the relay queue with per-user keys; tokens short-lived and device-bound; no third-party
    trackers; open source code (AGPL-3.0-or-later) open to review; incident contact <TBD>.
 Q: Data-subject rights?
 A: Access, rectification, erasure and portability are exercised on the device itself (export, delete); the relay holds nothing
    after pickup; requests to the contact above are answered within one month (GDPR Art. 12(3)).
