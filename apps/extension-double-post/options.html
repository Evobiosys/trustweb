When you press the publish button on one of these sites, this extension asks whether to open TrustWeb with your text. It reads the compose text only at that click, sends nothing anywhere and stores only these settings. The text travels in the address of the new tab (after the #), which browsers never send to a server; TrustWeb clears it at once.
